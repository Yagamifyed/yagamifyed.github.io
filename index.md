<p align="justify"> Hey there – thanks for visiting my website! My name is Levin Güver and I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the <b>University of Zurich</b>, where I am composing my habilitation on the philosophical foundations of criminal procedure law. I hold a PhD from <b>University College London</b>, where I was jointly supervised across the Law Faculty and Philosophy Department by <a href="https://www.ucl.ac.uk/laws/people/dr-mark-dsouza">Mark Dsouza</a> and <a href="https://www.ucl.ac.uk/philosophy/people/professor-john-hyman">John Hyman</a>, as well as a Bachelor of Law and Master of Law from the <b>University of Zurich</b>, the latter of which I graduated as valedictorian. I am also an active member of the <a href="https://idea-lab.uni-graz.at/de/unsere-forschenden/ethik-der-kuenstlichen-intelligenz/">IDea_Lab</a> (formerly known as the <a href="https://www.guiltymindslab.com/">Guilty Minds Lab</a>), and have spent some time at both the <b>MPI-CSL</b> and <b>Yale Law School</b>.</p>

<p align="justify"> My interests lie at the intersection of <u>law</u>, <u>philosophy</u>, and <u>psychology</u>, broadly construed. I am most passionate about the philosophical foundations of criminal law and am currently writing my habilitation on  <u>moral</u> and <u>epistemological</u> puzzles that beset the criminal process. I am also greatly interested in <u>action-theoretical</u> queries – my dissertation was on the concept of intention in and outside the law – and hold auxilliary interests in the philosophy of <u>blame and punishment</u> and (the ethics of) <u>artificial intelligence</u>. I further enjoy tackling jurisprudential questions from an <u>empirical angle</u> (see <a href="https://lawreview.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/03%20Tobia.pdf">here</a> here for a wonderful review article of what that looks like) and have done some work on biases afflicting <u>causal cognition</u> and, by extension, the law. 

<p align="justify"> In my free time I enjoy lifting weights, running, playing chess, video games, and reading manga.</p>
